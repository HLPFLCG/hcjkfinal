This is where I'll share the small things — the poems that didn't make the book, the thoughts that arrive at 2am, the moments between moments.

Writing has always been my way of making sense of the world. This journal is an extension of that — a quieter, more personal space.

## Why a journal?

Because some words need room to breathe. Because not everything fits neatly into a poem. Because I want you to know the person behind the pages.

I See You, I See Me was born from years of writing in the margins — on napkins, in voice memos at 3am, in the notes app during moments I couldn't look away from. This journal is the next chapter of that.

> some things are too true
> to say out loud —
> so i give them
> to the page instead

I'll be sharing new poems, reflections on the writing process, behind-the-scenes moments, and whatever else finds its way here.

Thank you for being here. Thank you for reading. Thank you for seeing me.

— Heather
